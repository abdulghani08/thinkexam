## 1. Getting Started

### 1.1 What You Need

- A smartphone or tablet with Android 8.0 or higher
- The Easy Exam app installed
- A Google account (your school email)
- Internet connection

### 1.2 Installing the App

1. Open Google Play Store on your device
2. Search for "Easy Exam"
3. Tap **Install**
4. Wait for the app to install
5. Tap **Open** to start the app

### 1.3 Signing In

1. Open the Easy Exam app
2. Tap **"Sign in with Google"**
3. Select your school Google account
4. Wait for the app to load
5. **Important:** Your account must be registered by your teacher first

### 1.4 If You Can't Sign In

If you see a message saying "Account not authorized":

- Tell your teacher
- Your teacher needs to register your account
- Wait for your teacher to confirm you're registered
- Try signing in again

Pull down on the screen to refresh and see new exams.

## 3. Taking an Exam

### 3.1 Starting an Exam

1. On your dashboard, find an available exam (green)
2. Tap on the exam
3. Read the exam instructions carefully
4. Check the exam details:
   - **Duration:** How much time you have
   - **Number of Questions:** How many questions
   - **Passing Grade:** Score needed to pass
5. Tap **"Start Exam"**
6. Confirm that you want to start

### 3.2 What Happens When You Start

- The timer starts counting down
- The first question appears
- You cannot leave the app during the exam
- Your answers are saved automatically

### 3.3 The Timer

- The timer is at the top of the screen
- It shows how much time is left
- When time is low (1 minute), it turns red
- When time runs out, your exam is submitted automatically

### 3.4 Important Warnings

⚠️ **Do NOT leave the app during the exam**
- If you leave the app, it counts as a violation
- After 3 violations, your exam is submitted automatically
- Only leave if it's an emergency

## 4. Answering Questions

### 4.1 Question Screen

Each question shows:

- **Question Number:** Which question you're on (e.g., "Question 3 of 20")
- **The Question:** What you need to answer
- **Answer Options:** Choices you can select
- **Navigation Buttons:** Next and Previous buttons

### 4.2 Answering a Question

1. Read the question carefully
2. Look at all the answer options
3. Tap the option you think is correct
4. The option will be highlighted when selected
5. Tap **"Next"** to go to the next question

### 4.3 Changing Your Answer

You can change your answer anytime before submitting:

1. Go back to the question
2. Tap a different option
3. Your new answer is saved automatically

### 4.4 Skipping a Question

If you don't know an answer:

1. Leave it blank
2. Go to the next question
3. Come back later if you have time
4. Use the question numbers to jump to it

### 4.5 Flagging for Review

If you want to review a question later:

1. Tap the **flag icon** (🚩)
2. The question is marked for review
3. You can see all flagged questions at the end

### 4.6 Navigating Between Questions

**Next Button:** Go to the next question
**Previous Button:** Go to the previous question
**Question Numbers:** Tap a number to jump to that question

### 4.7 Question Status Icons

- ✅ **Checkmark:** You answered this question
- ⭕ **Circle:** You haven't answered this question
- 🚩 **Flag:** You marked this for review

## 5. Submitting Your Exam

### 5.1 When to Submit

Submit your exam when:

- You have answered all questions
- The timer is about to run out
- You're done and ready to finish

### 5.2 Submitting Your Exam

1. Answer all the questions you can
2. Tap the **"Submit"** button
3. You'll see a summary:
   - How many questions you answered
   - How many you skipped
   - How many you flagged
4. Review the summary
5. If you want to change answers, tap **"Go Back"**
6. If you're ready, tap **"Confirm Submit"**
7. Wait for the exam to submit

### 5.3 After Submitting

- Your exam is sent to your teacher
- Your answers are scored automatically
- You'll see your results (if your teacher enabled it)
- You cannot change your answers after submitting

### 5.4 Auto-Submit

Your exam is submitted automatically if:

- The timer runs out (time is up)
- You leave the app too many times (3 violations)
- Your teacher closes the exam

## 6. Viewing Your Results

### 6.1 When Results Appear

Results appear right after you submit IF your teacher enabled "Show Results."

If you don't see results:
- Your teacher might review them first
- Results might be shown later
- Ask your teacher when results will be available

### 6.2 What Results Show

- **Your Score:** How many points you got
- **Percentage:** Your score as a percentage
- **Pass/Fail:** Whether you passed or failed
- **Correct Answers:** How many you got right
- **Wrong Answers:** How many you got wrong
- **Unanswered:** How many you skipped

### 6.3 Reviewing Your Answers

If your teacher enabled answer review:

1. After seeing your results
2. Tap **"Review Answers"**
3. You'll see:
   - Your answers
   - Correct answers
   - Explanations (if your teacher added them)
4. This helps you learn from your mistakes

## 8. Important Rules

### 8.1 During the Exam

✅ **DO:**
- Read questions carefully
- Take your time
- Answer all questions you can
- Ask your teacher for help if you have technical problems

❌ **DON'T:**
- Leave the app during the exam
- Try to take screenshots
- Ask other students for answers
- Use your phone for other things
- Close the app intentionally

### 8.2 Violations

A violation happens when:
- You leave the app (press home button)
- You try to take a screenshot
- You switch to another app

**What happens:**
- First violation: Warning message
- Second violation: Another warning
- Third violation: Your exam is submitted automatically

### 8.3 Academic Honesty

- Do your own work
- Don't copy from others
- Don't share your answers
- Be honest and fair

## 9. Tips for Success

### 9.1 Before the Exam

- **Get a good night's sleep**
- **Eat a healthy meal**
- **Charge your device**
- **Find a quiet place**
- **Have your device ready**

### 9.2 During the Exam

- **Read each question carefully**
- **Don't rush** - take your time
- **If you don't know, skip and come back**
- **Watch the timer** - don't run out of time
- **Check your answers** if you have time

### 9.3 Answering Questions

- **Eliminate wrong answers** first
- **Think about what you learned in class**
- **Use the process of elimination**
- **Don't leave questions blank** if you can help it
- **Trust your instincts**

### 9.10 Time Management

- **Spend more time on hard questions**
- **Don't spend too long on one question**
- **Skip hard questions and come back**
- **Keep an eye on the timer**
- **Submit a few minutes before time runs out**

## 10. Getting Help

### 10.1 Technical Problems

If the app isn't working:

1. **Check your internet connection**
2. **Close and reopen the app**
3. **Restart your device**
4. **Tell your teacher immediately**

### 10.2 During the Exam

If you have a problem during the exam:

1. **Raise your hand** (if in classroom)
2. **Tell your teacher or proctor**
3. **Don't try to fix it yourself**
4. **Your teacher will help you**

### 10.3 Account Problems

If you can't sign in:

1. **Tell your teacher**
2. **Make sure you're using the right Google account**
3. **Wait for your teacher to register your account**
4. **Try again later**

### 10.4 Questions About the Exam

If you don't understand a question:

1. **Read it again carefully**
2. **Skip it and come back**
3. **Don't ask other students**
4. **Ask your teacher** (if allowed)

## 12. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What happens if I run out of time?**
A: Your exam is submitted automatically with whatever answers you have.

**Q: Can I pause the exam and come back later?**
A: No, once you start, you must finish in one session.

**Q: What if I accidentally close the app?**
A: Open it again quickly. You might get a warning violation.

**Q: Can I see my answers after submitting?**
A: Only if your teacher enabled answer review.

**Q: What if my device runs out of battery?**
A: Tell your teacher immediately. They may be able to help.

**Q: Can I take the exam on a computer?**
A: No, students take exams on the Android app only.

**Q: What if I don't know an answer?**
A: Skip it and come back if you have time. Don't guess randomly.

**Q: How do I know if I passed?**
A: Your results will show Pass/Fail based on the passing grade.

**Q: Can I retake an exam if I fail?**
A: Only if your teacher allows it. Ask your teacher.

**Q: What if the app freezes?**
A: Tell your teacher immediately. Don't try to fix it yourself.
